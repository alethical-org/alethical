Which cities and counties get named infrastructure grants?

2025–2026 LEGISLATIVE SESSION · AS OF JUL 30, 2026
Share this answer
Copied
SHARE TO

Nineteen cities are named for infrastructure grants:

Cohasset Cold Spring Cook Crystal Dayton Freeport Fridley Independence International Falls Jordan Kandiyohi Karlstad Keewatin Lafayette Lake Benton Silver Lake South Haven Spicer St. Francis

Each grant is a separate amount tied to specific improvements. No counties are named for infrastructure grants directly.

HF 719 OMNIBUS Signed into Law

Statewide Capital Projects and Bonding Bill

Authorizes billions in state bond financing for construction and renovation projects across nearly every part of state government, plus grants to specific cities, counties, and other local entities for roads, water systems, public safety buildings, parks, and community facilities. It also adjusts some earlier funding commitments and temporarily reduces vehicle registration taxes.
