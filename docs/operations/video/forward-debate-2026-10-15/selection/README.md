# Shorts selection record (internal)

Codex's editorial selection from the full Forward Debate transcript, October 20, 2026: 41 ideas with
fixed IDs, as 29 transcript selects and 12 conditional reserves. Not 41 approved or publish-ready clips.

- [forward-debate-shorts-selects.md](forward-debate-shorts-selects.md): the record, unchanged except that
  2 links to files on a Mac now point at this folder's copies.
- [selects-data.json](selects-data.json): its data (cues, context, bounds).

How to read it:
- Times use the **published** (YouTube) clock. The recording runs 39.06 s later.
- Titles are paraphrase proposals, not quotations and not approved copy.
- "Find in captions" anchors are machine-text search aids, never text to publish.
- Each entry's "Keep", "Before an edit" and a reserve's hold are conditions. A clip's brief carries
  them and records how each was resolved from picture, audio or sources; an unresolved hold keeps the
  clip a reserve.
- An edited reading transcript is not the complete speech; candidates' statements stay claims.
