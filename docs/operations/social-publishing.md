# Writing and managing Alethical social posts

This guide owns Alethical-specific social writing and publication practice. Video editing belongs
in the [separate video guide](video/shorts-guide.md); website and YouTube publication belongs in
[blog-publishing.md](blog-publishing.md).

## Read both sources and keep learning

Before drafting, posting or editing, read this guide and the
[shared social publishing guide](https://github.com/euglopi/tool-settings/blob/main/shared/guides/social-publishing.md).
For video, also read the
[shared upload limits](https://github.com/euglopi/tool-settings/blob/main/shared/guides/social-video-upload-limits.md).
Alethical's voice, services, people, accounts, event records and approved choices belong in this
repository. General platform behavior and reusable methods belong in tool settings. Each task saves
new lessons in the owning document and updates affected links before completion. A correction belongs
in the rule it changes; a case-specific choice stays in the post's record. Do not copy the same rule
into both repos or turn an untested guess into a standing requirement. Apply each evolved rule to
all relevant platforms under the shared guide. Assess each destination's actual capabilities and
record evidence for Alethical-specific exceptions here; scope does not authorize extra posting or
other account actions.

## Write the result, its meaning and what comes next

- Lead with a concrete reason to read or watch. Combine the event's supported outcome with the
  inspiration it offers and a specific, approved next step readers can look forward to.
- Use plain, welcoming language that lets people disagree and still work together. Keep candidate
  statements and audience reactions attributed. Praise for a debate does not establish agreement
  with every candidate position or support from the whole audience.
- Keep direct quotes exact and sourced. Report supplied audience feedback as feedback, not a poll.
  Do not turn “some attendees” into “everyone” or infer party membership from an audience reaction.
- Describe planned services as plans. Use the [campaign services guide](../product-onboarding/services-guide.md)
  and the current approved announcement to distinguish available help from future offerings.
- Give each platform a complete version suited to its current limits. Preserve facts, qualifications,
  required links and the reader's next action while shortening. A reference to 6 platforms is not a
  permanent platform list; identify the intended accounts for the actual request.
- Link the full replay or article where readers can watch or read it. Keep post text, video,
  thumbnail and link preview consistent. Retain useful context without repeating what the media
  already makes clear. Resolve routine size and format constraints using the shared guide's
  source-preserving adaptation method; keep candidate names, faces and approved artwork intact.

## Link people and organizations deliberately

Link named participants to their verified profiles or pages. Use the platform's clickable name
mention where supported. A typed name, an unselected `@name` or a Markdown label is not proof of a
working mention. Follow the shared guide's identity and saved-post checks.

Check for each candidate's campaign account separately on every platform. Use verified campaign
pages in the introduction and verified personal profiles in the closing thank-you when both types
are available. A campaign page is distinct from a personal profile or unrelated employer; verify
the role of each destination and never substitute an employer for a campaign.

If no matching campaign account is verified after a reasonable search on that platform, omit that
type under the approved fallback without asking again. Follow the shared guide's bounded search,
then stop retrying without new evidence. Record that it was not found, not that it does not exist;
results on 1 platform do not settle another. When only a personal profile is linked,
link it once at the first mention and leave repeated names, including the closing thank-you, plain.
Do not add repetitive name mentions merely to fit both account types.

If neither a personal nor campaign social account is verified after a reasonable search, use the
candidate's name followed by the verified official campaign website once. Apply this fallback on
every platform that supports it; do not invent a handle or substitute an employer. Adapt the format
to the actual limits while preserving required facts and links. If those requirements cannot fit
in a supported format, make the specific conflict explicit before publication.

When discussing joining the Forward Independence Coalition, place the
[coalition candidates link](https://forwardcoalition.com/candidates) beside that discussion. Do not
leave the coalition unnamed or rely on a separate replay link to explain the affiliation.

## Publish and maintain the existing post

Use the chosen connected publishing service within its approved free-account scope. Facebook and
LinkedIn announcements remain with Zernio. Adeli is the selected route for the 3 connected Alethical
accounts: TikTok **Alethical.com**, YouTube **@alethical** and Bluesky **@alethical.com**. Recheck
connections before use; this is not authorization for a paid upgrade or additional accounts.

Handle 1 platform at a time and verify its saved result before moving to the next. Confirm the
platform, Alethical account, approved text, media edition, links, visibility and requested timing.
Approval for 1 destination does not approve every connected account. A revision updates the existing
post; it does not create another copy. On YouTube, use the existing full replay rather than upload a
duplicate, and confirm the requested format and existing channel content before publishing.

Use the platform's own editor for a needed feature that the connector cannot complete, such as
resolving a particular name mention. Read the live post before editing so that later platform edits
are not overwritten by stale connector text. Preserve existing names, destinations, coalition link,
replay link and media preview unless the requested change affects them.

Keep a post record with the approved text, intended accounts, media edition, profile destinations,
connector post ID when available, live address, schedule/status, changes and completed checks.
Credentials and authentication data stay out of the record. Publishing is complete only when the
saved post shows the intended text and links on the correct account.

## Keep significant milestones visible

Significant milestones should help new visitors understand Alethical's work. When preparing a post,
suggest an appropriate pin, featured placement or equivalent on each relevant platform using the
[shared placement guidance](https://github.com/euglopi/tool-settings/blob/main/shared/guides/social-publishing.md#keep-significant-posts-easy-to-find).
Assess the actual account, format, current placements and limits before suggesting an addition,
reorder or replacement. A useful existing milestone remains when the platform supports adding more.

When a slot is scarce or the collection is full, compare what visitors would gain from the new
milestone and suggest a replacement only when it serves them better. Do not remove a featured post
merely because a new one is added, set an automatic expiry, or delete the underlying post. LinkedIn
features accumulate within its documented limit; the shared guide owns the current platform details.
Future posting work includes this placement recommendation, not automatic permission to publish,
pin, feature, reorder or remove material on another account.

The Forward Debate replay announcement is a significant milestone. Pinning its Facebook post and
featuring its LinkedIn post are authorized for this announcement; saved placement results belong
with the post records. This approval does not extend to other platforms or future announcements.
